    How to interpret the evidence

    Government surveys describe defined populations and observation periods. Experimental research evaluates particular tasks and conditions. Technical documentation explains methods and operational choices. Provider descriptions establish what a provider says it offers; they do not independently verify business results.

    The source directory matches the article. The chart data CSV retains the values and populations behind the three original graphics.
